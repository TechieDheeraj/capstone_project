# Shipping policy — Copper Kettle Co.

## Carriers {#carriers}
We ship with two carriers. SwiftShip tracking numbers start with "SS-". Parcelo tracking numbers start with "PC-".
SwiftShip has an online tracking API. Parcelo has only a web page for tracking.

## Delivery times {#times}
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days after we ship. Orders ship within 2 business days after payment.
The estimated delivery date (ETA) on the tracking page is an estimate, not a promise.

## Order status {#status}
paid = we received the payment and the order waits to ship. shipped = the carrier has the parcel. delivered = the carrier delivered it.

## Address problems {#address}
If the carrier holds a parcel because of an address problem, the customer can contact the carrier to fix the address, or ask us for a refund if the parcel is late.
