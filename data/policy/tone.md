# How we write to customers — Copper Kettle Co.

## Style {#style}
Write short, warm and clear e-mails. Use the customer's first name. Say what happened, what we will do, and when.
Do not promise anything that the policy does not allow. Do not blame the customer or the carrier.
Sign every e-mail as "The Copper Kettle team".

## Bad news {#bad-news}
When the answer is no, say no clearly and give the reason from the policy in one sentence. Then offer the best thing that the policy allows.
